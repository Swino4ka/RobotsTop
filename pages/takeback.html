Account recovery

About account recovery

Recover account

Sends a recovery link to the main email of the account. The link resets the password, removes any recovery email and cancels any pending email change. It is valid for 1 hour.

Main email priority

The main email always has priority. Whoever controls it can always reclaim the account, even if someone else knows the password or has added a recovery email.

When to use

Use this if your password was changed without your consent, or if a recovery email you did not add appears on your account. A normal password reset is enough for a forgotten password.
